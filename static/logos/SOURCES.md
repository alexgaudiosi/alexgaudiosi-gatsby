# Company wordmarks

Brand assets used to identify companies in Alex Gaudiosi's portfolio. Trademarks belong to their respective owners. Files are hosted locally; no logo API or visitor tracking is used.

- Salomon: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Salomon_logo_2022.svg (source: Salomon).
- WPP: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WPP_2020_logo.svg (source: WPP).
- VML: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:VML_agency_logo.png — raster wordmark with symbol, not the standalone symbol.
- Finisterre: https://finisterre.com/cdn/shop/t/665/assets/finisterre-logo.svg?v=51705132978227857211665393262
- Beauty Bay: https://www.freelogovectors.net/beauty-bay-logo/ — raster wordmark.
- Atomic: full wordmark SVG extracted from the footer of https://www.atomic.com/ (not its standalone A symbol).
- Armada: https://www.armadasnow.com/cdn/shop/files/Logo.svg?v=1769987109&width=600

- Shopify Plus: https://seeklogo.com/vector-logo/393730/shopify-plus — raster wordmark including the Shopify bag and full Shopify Plus name.
- Essity: https://www.essity.com/assets/img/logo-essity.svg — official wordmark.
